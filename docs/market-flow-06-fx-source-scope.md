# FX PvP source scope

The FX PvP route is based on the CLSSettlement service overview cited in the
source registry. It is a schematic of linked payment instructions, multilateral
netting, eligible-currency funding, and settlement confirmation.

It does not show an executable FX trade, a universal currency-pair route,
participant balances, prices, or legal settlement finality for a specific
transaction. Coverage, membership, currencies, and operating times remain
subject to the cited service's documented scope.
