# Asset-backed securitization lifecycle

## Scope

This guide introduces a high-level asset-pool, SPV, tranche, and waterfall structure. It is different from an individual bond issuance or a syndicated-loan commitment.

## Stages

1. Define the asset-pool category.
2. Set the SPV and tranche terms.
3. Issue the securities and close the transaction.
4. Collect cash flows and apply the documented waterfall.

## Evidence boundary

The SEC ABS issuance page provides the public issuance and Regulation AB disclosure context. It does not establish the legal treatment, pool performance, rating, price, loss, cash amount, or status of a particular securitization.
