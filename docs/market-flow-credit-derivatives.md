# Credit derivatives credit-event lifecycle

## Scope

This guide models a conditional documentation path after a possible credit event. It is separate from general OTC clearing, margin, and CCP clearing-member default management.

## Stages

1. Submit a request and check the contract documentation.
2. Apply the relevant Determinations Committee process.
3. Publish event-specific auction terms if an auction is held.
4. Settle according to the applicable contract method and terms.

## Evidence boundary

ISDA documentation describes the Determinations Committee and auction-settlement framework. It does not prove that any real credit event occurred or determine a reference entity, price, recovery, settlement amount, or legal outcome for a particular trade.
